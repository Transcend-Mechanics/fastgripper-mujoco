First-party licensing is described in LICENSE. Bundled robot assets keep
their original license texts and attribution:

- UR5e: assets/ur5e/LICENSE, assets/ur5e/README.md and its source manifest.
- UR3e: assets/ur3e/LICENSE, assets/ur3e/UPSTREAM_README.md,
