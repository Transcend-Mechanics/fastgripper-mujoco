## What a license cannot establish

In the United States, copyright does not itself protect the mechanical or
functional aspects of a useful article, and copyright in a drawing does not
automatically create exclusive manufacturing rights over the depicted
mechanism. [U.S. Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/register/va-useful.html).
Confidential know-how also needs actual secrecy measures; publicly exposed
geometry cannot be made secret by labeling it proprietary.
[USPTO trade-secret guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/ip-policy/trade-secret-policy).

The distribution therefore includes simplified simulation geometry and omits
manufacturing source. Those models still expose their visible shape and
modeled dimensions. The custom terms express intended permissions; they are
not a guarantee against copying or a substitute for review of patent,
trade-secret, contract and other applicable rights. Have qualified counsel
review the final accessory/manufacturing boundary for the relevant jurisdictions.
